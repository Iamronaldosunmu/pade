## Overview

A night-ground page for a Friday-night live event in Lagos. Light bands may interrupt the dark ground, but dark is the default.

## Colors

`buzz` red is reserved for the live buzzer state. Do not use it for other emphasis. `gold` marks the accented letters in the wordmark and the highlighted phrase in headlines. `ink-muted` is for secondary text on `ground`.

## Typography

`display` carries headlines, `text` carries body copy, and `time` carries clocks, labels and chips.
